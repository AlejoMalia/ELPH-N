### Abstract

We set out a complete, costed and falsifiable theoretical framework for the physical
transport of a **closed volume** between two stations, together with a metrological
protocol that certifies that what arrives is what departed. The declared architecture is
the **complete and physical** transport of a whole human inside a **bubble/object**,
**without cutting and without reading the interior**: the interior is a cabin that is
never modified, because the laws of nature maintain it; what is transported and certified
is the **shell**, and the human travels inside as **cargo**.

Excluded from the first day: wormholes, speculative quantum formalism, and magical
dematerialisation. Nothing here proposes a new mechanism of nature. What is proposed is
**metrology, protocol, and evidence accounting**.

The framework rests on an agreement contract `A = √(Ω_imp/Ω_act)` evaluated against the
**nearest** impostor, with an explicitly declared **dead zone**, and a false-acceptance
rate reported with its own sample size under the rule of three. It yields a set of hard
limits that were derived, not assumed: a **5.03 m ceiling** on bubble size, a **1 g**
declared acceleration, an obligation that **every act be exo-atmospheric**, and an
operating principle — *the door is never the punishment* — that separates delivery from
certification.

Across eight axes of completeness, the seven **statement** axes stand at 100 % and the
eighth — **MEASURED** — stands at **0 %**. The framework is at **87.5 %**, and the missing
12.5 points are a single axis costing **€1,674**.

> This document is a **pre-registered protocol for third parties to refute**, not a report
> of results.

## 1. Genesis: why a neuron, and why an elephant

The programme began with a metaphor and survived it. The question was whether the
information content of a nervous system could be transferred, and the elephant in the
title is the absurdity of the scale involved: fitting something enormous through something
very small.

That framing was wrong, and correcting it was the single most productive act of the whole
programme.

### 1.1 The globe

**A connectome is not a brain being measured. It is a GLOBE.**

| object in the graph | what it is |
|:--|:--|
| a node | **a city** — neuron A = **London**, neuron B = **New York** |
| a whole connectome | **a country or a continent** |
| the set of connectomes | **the planet** |
| an edge | which places can be reached from a place |
| the weight | coupling between places |
| the impostor | **a wrong destination address** — never "another individual" |

We speak always of a **human body**, never of a *nervous system*. Measuring on worm or
fly tissue is a **test bench for space**, not neuroscience: the biological substrate is
the vehicle, not the subject.

And **a teleportation is measured between two PLACES IN THE SAME SPACE** — London → New
York — never between two individuals. Comparing two distinct individuals **is not a
teleportation**.

The vocabulary of "measuring brains" narrowed the design of experiments for weeks without
any number demanding it. Under the space framing, one experiment showed that the act
certifies equally well in human cardiac tissue as in nervous tissue — band 8.97× against
9.37× — and the largest declared limit of the programme fell. It is an experiment that
would never have been designed under the old frame.

The same correction had already been made once: **the synapse is the MECHANISM to emulate
as a vehicle, not the object of study.** Same shape of error, same remedy.

### 1.3 The closed-route episode

At one point the programme spent two days comparing incommensurable magnitudes, and at

### 1.4 TRIADA and MATE

**TRIADA** — before spending computation:

1. **INVENTORY** — is it already measured, is it already on disk?
2. **MATHEMATICS** — does a bound answer it without simulating? **Written BEFORE running**,
   with numerical predictions P1/P2/P3.
3. **MATE** — what computation can be reused?

**MATE** — *Methodology of Advance by Strategic Tension*, created by the investigator from
the projection of a mate-in-three in chess. Five rules:

- **R1 · conservation of tension.** A line that releases the tension is abandoned even if
  it looks safe.
- **R2 · invariance of the rules.** The rules of the game do not change to suit the plan.
- **R3 · projection of the last three.** Before moving, the final three moves are named.
- **R4 · advance through critical squares.** Progress is measured by squares that matter,
  not by moves made.
- **R5 · mate ≠ theatre.** A position that looks winning and is not, is a loss.

> **TRIADA is the opening and MATE is the endgame. TRIADA is R3 turned into procedure.**

## 2. The phases of the programme

The programme passed through the following stages, each of which ended when a number, not
an opinion, closed it.

**Phase 1 · the metaphor.** Connectomics and the synapse as an information channel. Closed
by the globe correction: the substrate is a vehicle.

**Phase 2 · thermodynamic and informational limits.** Bekenstein-type bounds on what a
reading architecture would require. Closed by the decision to **not read**.

**Phase 3 · non-cloning.** Parfit's paradox and the biological consequences of any
architecture that copies. Closed for the same reason: with φ = 1 and no reading, the
question does not arise.

**Phase 4 · baryonic transit.** The mass must travel. This is the point at which the
programme stops being about information and starts being about metrology.

**Phase 5 · the act of the object.** Multi-body matter, the `3n − 6` spatial law, and the
minimum number of constraints needed to fix a configuration.

**Phase 6 · closed-volume custody.** The shell becomes the certified object and the
interior becomes cargo. This is the declared architecture.

**Phase 7 · the verification engine.** The contract, the impostor, the floor, GATE-0, and
common-mode cancellation.

**Phase 8 · the protocol.** The pipeline, the limbos, the abort semantics, the operating
economics — the content of §5-bis through §5-quater.

**A note on what was dropped.** An earlier architecture assumed *destruction plus
reconstruction*, forced by dose considerations. That architecture is **superseded**: it
belonged to a frame in which the interior had to be read. With no reading, there is
nothing to destroy and nothing to reconstruct.

## 3. The formal framework

The framework is a set of statements, each carrying its evidentiary status, its units, a
falsifying experiment, and that experiment's price. The complete formulary is Annex A;
what follows are the load-bearing results.

### 3.1 The contract

```
Ω  = 1 − corr                              disagreement
A  = √( Ω_imp / Ω_act )                    band, against the NEAREST impostor
dA/A = ½ √( u_imp² + u_act² )              dead zone
```

**The contract exists if and only if `A > 1 + dA`.** Two points deserve emphasis because
both were defects that had to be found and fixed:

- The impostor is the **nearest**, not the median. Taking the median of the impostor
  distribution flatters the band; the tail is what matters, exactly as in biometric
  practice.
- Without a dead zone, `A = 1.05` was being reported as a contract. **It is a tie.**

Measured over 3·10⁶ impostors: `Ω_act = 8.16·10⁻⁴`, nearest impostor `4.99·10⁻²`,
**`A = 7.82`**, `d′ = 5.5`.

### 3.2 The false-acceptance rate, reported honestly

```
FAR ≤ 3/k                                  rule of three (Hanley & Lippman-Hand, 1983)
```

A rate is never reported without its **k**. Over 3·10⁶ impostors with zero acceptances,
**FAR ≤ 10⁻⁶**. The same discipline applies to the operational failure rate: *zero deaths
in 37* means **at most one in twelve**, not zero.

### 3.3 The act is material, not informational

```
ψ = g · K⁴ / A⁴  =  1.03·10⁻⁴
```

The act is **9,713 times more material than informational**. This single number is what
licenses the whole framework to be metrology rather than information theory.

### 3.4 The Non-Fusion Theorem

With φ = 1 and no reading, the *Brundlefly* failure — two configurations mixing — **is
impossible by construction**: there is no pattern to mix. This is not a safety measure. It
is a consequence of refusing to read.

### 3.5 The law of the map: `υ ≥ L`

```
υ ≥ L                                      υ/L converges to 0.6
```

**υ is the size of the OPERATION that formed the part.** Point-by-point milling: 100–500 mm.
Mould, press, deep-draw, hydroform: the whole part.

> **The two shells must be formed in ONE operation with the same tooling.**

This eliminates welds as a failure mode, and it is the most falsifiable and most
potentially novel claim in the framework. It is also, at €25, the cheapest thing in it to
test.

### 3.6 GATE-0 — the veto the architecture hangs from

```
D(r) = C · r^α                             structure function over paired fiducials

VETO         if α < 0.35                   reliable at 98.7 %
PASS         if α ≥ 0.95  AND  D(176 mm) ≤ 35 µm
INTERMEDIATE otherwise                     NOT sufficient to launch the act
```

α ≈ 1 means the residual error field is **smooth**, and a local affine kills it as
`(r/L)²`. α ≈ 0 means the error is **independent per site**, and the hierarchy of
references does not work at all. One exponent answers *is this error correctable?*

Four defects had to be repaired here, and they are instructive:

- The amplitude criterion was originally a fixed threshold on `C`. Since `α` is a **fitted
  parameter**, the units of `C` change with every fit: the threshold was **dimensionally
  invalid**, and it never once bit in 1,800 fields. It is now `D(r₀)` in micrometres at a
  reference separation.
- `α` was estimated by ordinary least squares, whose breakdown point is `1/n`. A single
  50 µm speck on 25 fiducials dragged α from 0.974 to 0.765 and turned PASS into
  INTERMEDIATE. The estimator is now **Theil-Sen** (breakdown 29.3 %).
- The fiducial plate was a **grid**, which is blind to the 4θ error a three-axis mill
  actually produces. It is now **five rings**.
- A **perfect** field produced α = NaN and a verdict of "NO DATA": the instrument failed in
  the best possible case, which is the worst place to fail. A field below the reader's
  floor is not absence of data — it is a perfectly smooth field, and that is α = 1 by
  construction.

The declaration that GATE-0 is a **reliable veto (98.7 %) and a poor pass (46 %)** is kept
explicit, because a test that is honest about which of its two answers to trust is rarer
than it should be.

### 3.7 The percentile that lies at your sample size

```
PCT_INV = 100 · (1 − 1/2n)
```

A fixed percentile threshold misrepresents detection power at small `n`. Using p98 with
n = 50 detected **10 %** of the cases where 90 % had been declared. The percentile must be
tied to the sample size.

## 4. The phase diagram: where this framework applies

The framework is not universal and says so. Three boundaries delimit it.

**By size.** `Ω ∝ 1/L` makes a larger cabin *easier* to certify, while `α` decreasing with
`L` makes GATE-0 *impossible*. The two effects are opposed, and `υ ≥ L` comes out of the
crossing. The hard ceiling is computed in §5-ter.4: **L ≤ 5.03 m**.

**By mass and energy.** `t_wall ∝ L`, `M_shell ∝ L³`, `E ∝ M ∝ L³`. From 30 cm to 3 m the
shell goes from 9 to 888 kg — **×99 of energy for the same trip in the same time**.

**By distance.** The transit-time law `t = 2√(d/a)` is valid only while the peak velocity
stays sub-escape. At 1 g this holds to **12,786 km**; at 9 g only to **1,421 km**. Beyond
that, the profile is not a hop but a departure from Earth, and the law does not model the
required coast phase.

# 5. THE TIME FACTOR · nothing is instantaneous, and the trip is rarely the longest part

## 5.1 Two separable terms

```
t_total = t_handling(m)  +  t_transit(d)
```

The first term depends on **mass** and on the certification work; the second on
**distance**. They are separable, and the surprise is which dominates.

## 5.2 The three formulas of time

```
t_transit = 2 √( d / a )              a = g_sustained · 9.81
t_floor   = 10 re-placements, or k = 3 control points if inherited
t_act     = t_pipeline + t_transit
```

**Time is set by the OCCUPANT, not by the mass.** Mass sets the **energy**, which scales
as `L³`.

## 5.3 The crossing distance is a staircase

For a person, the crossing distance — where transit starts costing more than certifying —
is **358,983 km**. The Moon is at 384,400 km. **The Moon falls almost exactly where the
journey begins to cost more than certifying it.**

*(Honesty note: this figure is sensitive to the handling model at the 10 % level. The
conclusion holds either way because the Moon sits inside that margin, but `t_handling` is
the term most worth measuring, and it is marked DEC rather than DER for that reason.)*

## 5.4 What this section closes

> **Nothing is instantaneous. The time of a teleportation is not set by the destination:
> it is set by the mass and by the certificate, and the destination only adds a term
> which, below the Moon, is the minor one.** An act is **fast** when the cargo fits in one
> hand and the destination is far. It is **slow** when a crane is needed, **even if the
> destination is one kilometre away.**

## 5-bis.2 What the pipeline found

**First: 9 of 17 abort branches had no destination.** The framework had been attacked six
times on the physical steps and **not once on the control flow**. The nine orphan branches
all fall after sealing — that is, **with a person inside** — while the eight before it
return to S0 and are trivial. Sealing was not merely the administrative point of no
return: it was **the boundary between two different abort regimes**.

**Second: the battery did not close.** It had been sized for the act (3.4 min) and the
pipeline lasts twenty times longer: 105 W × 77 min = **135 Wh against 124 of battery**. The
answer is not more battery — the cabin runs on an **umbilical** while in station, and the
battery only covers S12→S16: 7.4 min, 13 Wh, 9.6× margin. That forces a **hot-disconnect
power penetration** that the penetration count had missed.

**Third, and the one that matters: THE DOOR IS NEVER THE PUNISHMENT.**

- **Before commit there is rollback.** The act did not happen; the person leaves the way
  they came in. Read A gains a **hard limit of three retries** — without a limit it was a
  loop with an occupant inside — and a pressure abort descends **at the same rate** at
  which it rose.
- **After commit there is no rollback.** Delivery is always fulfilled; the only thing that
  can fail is the certificate. The five failures from S15 to S21 all resolve **the same
  way — by opening**: arrival out of tolerance opens and does not certify; reconciliation
  that does not match opens and leaves the act **in dispute**; a sealed seed that fails to
  decrypt opens and leaves the act **null** — it does not contaminate the rate; failed exit
  biometrics opens and credentials manually, because locking someone in over a 10⁻³ FRR is
  unacceptable.

The framework had six criteria for denying a certificate and **none** for denying an
opening, because denying an opening is not an option of the system. It was true, and that
is why nobody had written it. **What is implicit cannot be audited.**

# 5-ter. WHAT THE FRAMEWORK LEARNED AFTER THE PIPELINE

## 5-ter.1 The acceleration was a decision, and it was the wrong one

The framework declared 9 g for hundreds of decisions without anyone asking why. Checking a
table of real times revealed that `E = m·a·d` is **linear in acceleration**: dropping to
1 g multiplies transit by 3 and **divides energy by 9**.

And transit does not matter. The act is **97 % paperwork**: London–Berlin goes from 3 to
10 minutes of flight, and from 2.0 to 2.1 hours door to door. In exchange, at 1 g the neck
criterion disappears — an infant carries **25 %** of its mass in the head against an
adult's 7 % — along with aspiration on vomiting, the anti-g suit, the reclined posture,
loose objects as projectiles, and nearly the whole medical fitness list.

> **Seven of the thirteen occupant gaps did not come from the act: they came from the
> acceleration.**

The reach of the transit law also multiplies by 9, since `v = √(a·d)`: from 1,421 km to
**12,786 km**. **Declared: 1 g.**

## 5-ter.2 The contradiction hidden for 715 decisions

The framework demanded `D(176 mm) ≤ 35 µm` of form and sent the shell through the
atmosphere at 8–9 km/s. **The heating was never calculated.** At 1,200 K the expansion of
176 mm of steel is 2,534 µm: **72 times the budget**. And both standard remedies make it
worse — an **ablative** shield is consumed **by changing shape**, and a tiled shield adds
joints that kill `υ ≥ L` and bring down GATE-0.

It was resolved without materials, by two observations:

1. **The criterion is not ΔT: it is PERMANENT deformation.** Thermal expansion is
   reversible and the destination floor is established *after* cooling, so the only thing
   that breaks the floor is creep. For steel that is **T ≤ 450 °C**, not 35 µm.
2. **Velocity and density can never both be high.** Radiative equilibrium at 450 °C gives
   a 12.4 kW/m² ceiling, and Sutton-Graves turns that into a maximum velocity per altitude:
   0.42 km/s at sea level, 3.08 at 85 km, 4.80 at 100 km.

The 3.0 km/s that London–Berlin needs at 1 g therefore require being above **85 km**. The
price is **4.4 minutes** of ascent and descent. **Every act is exo-atmospheric**, and the
profile is designed with a heat-flux ceiling rather than a velocity ceiling. It is the
max-Q management of any launch vehicle: a solved problem we had not looked at.

## 5-ter.3 The powers, and the door

The powers are separated: **A builds the act, B judges it.** B confirms **twice** —
reservation and signature — and at commit **waives its vote**. The occupant turned out to
be the **third vote**: a veto of their own up to commit, none after. A and B must be
**independent signatories**, or the four eyes are two eyes twice.

| only A | only B |
|:--|:--|
| propose, weigh, inspect, board | reserve or **refuse without stating cause** |
| **seal** (requires a live reservation from B) | **verify the signature** (second veto) |
| establish the origin floor | brake and receive |
| read and sign the message | establish its floor |
| pressurise · **launch** | apply the six criteria |
| abort below 30 % of route | **OPEN THE DOOR** — A cannot prevent it |

Neither may open the blinding (the system does, with the sealed seed), abort above 30 %,
or annul an act — the register annuls it, not a will. **Reconciliation is the only joint
action.**

## 5-ter.4 The hard limits that appeared on costing

- **The bubble does not exceed ~5.03 m** (4–6 seats). With α = 1 the field's disagreement
  grows linearly and the detection floor does not: they cross there. It does not matter,
  because `E ∝ L³ ∝ N` makes **energy per person flat** — there is no economy of scale to
  lose by splitting.
- **Stations may differ.** Dimension enters no formula of the contract, and B's floor may
  be up to **7.8×** worse than A's. Route asymmetry **is paid in time, not in g**.
- **Real cadence is 8.2 acts/day**, not 18.7: nobody had counted who cleans the cabin.
- **Fatigue is not the limit.** Retirement at 50,000 cycles (8.4 years), inspection every
  5,000 (10 months), bounded by the six-month calendar already declared.

## 5-ter.5 Two advantages nobody had written, and the barrier nobody had seen

**Radiation dose scales with time, and the act is 50–100× faster.** A Hohmann transfer to
Mars is 468 mSv — **78 % of an astronaut's career limit, in one trip** — against **6.3 mSv**
for the act. It is the only quantity in the framework where haste **gives** safety. And the
profile accelerates continuously, so **there is no microgravity**: artificial gravity for
free, the whole way.

The barrier is elsewhere, and it is economic. The insurance premium does not need a
measured rate: it needs a **bound**, and the same rule of three the framework uses for the
FAR provides one. With the 37 reference acts, `P ≤ 3/37 = 8.1 %`, and against a statistical
value of life of €4 M the minimum premium is **€324,000 per act: 270 times the tariff**.

> **The framework is uninsurable today — not because it is dangerous, but because it is
> unknown.** For the premium to fall to 10 % of the tariff requires **~112,000 acts without
> a single loss**. It is the hardest barrier in the programme, and **it can only be torn
> down by operating.**

# 5-quater. THE ACT AS A SERVICE

## 5-quater.1 Who operates it

The four-eyes rule requires **two signatories per station**, and not any two: **a
metrologist**, who signs the functional verification of the thirteen instruments and
judges R², α and the floor, and **an operations lead**, who signs weighing, inspection,
boarding, sealing and commit.

**Four people per act, two per station.** It cannot go down to three: removing one breaks
the four eyes, and *A builds / B judges* forbids the same person covering both stations
even if they were alone. Nor is there one more: **there is no pilot** — nobody flies the
capsule — there is no cabin crew, and there is **no blinding operator**, because the
system opens it. The physician does not attend each act: fitness is certified by a
**validity window**, as with an aeronautical medical certificate.

Of the 24 pipeline states, **only four admit a human decision**: reservation, sealing,
signature verification and commit. Everything else is machine, procedure, or **forbidden
to decide**.

> **The panel is not flown: it is witnessed.** A Falcon 9 carries 20–30 people in the
> control room because there are decisions in flight. Here there are none: L4 has no
> controller. **The framework's worst safety defect is its greatest staffing advantage.**

Maintenance is not a team: cleaning and inter-act inspection are done by the operating
shift, the periodic work is an external contract of ~194 h/year — external for the same
reason the signatories are independent — and the in-house part is half a post for
software, sealed registry and keys. **0.4 FTE.**

But **staffing is set by shift coverage, not by the act**: two posts × 5,840 h/year is
**14.2 FTE** per station pair. **The act needs 4 people; the service needs 15.**

## 7. Conclusions

**1.** A framework was built that is **completely posed, dimensioned, costed and
resisted**, and that is **not measured**. Both halves of that sentence are the result.

**2.** The load-bearing claims are metrological, not physical. The programme proposes no
new mechanism of nature, and where it is worth anything outside its own premise, it is
worth it as method: `υ ≥ L` as an acceptance criterion, GATE-0 as a correctability veto,
and the **limbo catalogue** as a safety-engineering tool.

**3.** Several of the framework's own constraints turned out to be decisions rather than
constraints. **9 g was the clearest case:** it bought seven minutes that the paperwork
absorbs and cost ×9 in energy, ×27 in heat flux, /9 in reach, and seven of the thirteen
occupant gaps.

**4.** The control flow, not the physics, held the largest gaps. Nine of seventeen abort
branches had no destination, all of them with a person sealed inside, and the resolution —
**the door is never the punishment** — had been true all along and unwritten precisely
because it was true. **What is implicit cannot be audited.**

**5.** The economics are worse than the kinematics and the kinematics are better than
expected. The act beats the aeroplane door to door at any distance and beats the train
beyond 200 km; it is 5–8× the price in passenger terms and **2–3× in freight**; and it is
**uninsurable at today's evidence level by a factor of 270**.

**6.** The single most valuable action available to this programme costs **€25**.
